# CareerVault Complete Job Switch System

## Product Overview

CareerVault is a downloadable digital toolkit designed for working professionals with 1-5 years of experience who want to switch to better jobs, companies, roles, or salaries.

**Target Price:** ₹3,999  
**Primary Market:** India  
**Product Format:** Downloadable digital toolkit

## Core Promise

"Organise your entire job-switching process—from improving your professional profile and finding opportunities to clearing interviews and negotiating your next offer."

## Eight Commercial Modules

1. **Module 01: Resume Mastery System** - ATS optimisation, 15 editable resume templates, achievement-writing frameworks
2. **Module 02: Professional Profile Optimisation** - LinkedIn and Naukri optimisation, headlines, summaries, experience templates
3. **Module 03: Job Search Operating System** - Excel application tracker, company research, recruiter outreach templates
4. **Module 04: Interview Preparation Vault** - 200 interview questions, behavioural preparation, STAR workbook, evaluation scorecards
5. **Module 05: Salary and Offer Negotiation System** - Negotiation scripts, CTC vs take-home calculator, offer comparison
6. **Module 06: AI Career Assistant Toolkit** - 100 tested prompts, resume improvement workflows, job description matching
7. **Module 07: 90-Day Job Switch Programme** - Career assessment, skills-gap analysis, 30/60/90-day roadmap
8. **Module 08: Career Advancement Toolkit** - Portfolio presentation, professional communication, networking frameworks

## Six Career Tracks

- **Track A: Technology and IT** - Software engineering, QA and testing, Cloud and DevOps, Data roles, IT support, Technical operations
- **Track B: Business Analysis** - Business Analyst, Associate Business Analyst, Process Analyst, Business Operations Analyst
- **Track C: Product and Project Management** - Product Analyst, Associate Product Manager, Product Manager, Project Coordinator, Project Manager
- **Track D: Finance** - Financial Analyst, Accounts and finance operations, FP&A, Reporting and analysis
- **Track E: Sales and Marketing** - Business Development, Sales, Digital Marketing, Marketing Analyst, Account Management
- **Track F: Management and Operations** - Operations Analyst, Operations Manager, HR and people operations, Business Operations
