# Task 012 Privacy and Operations Review

## Items Requiring Owner/Professional Review

1. **Indian business identity** — GSTIN, business registration, contact details needed for invoices.
2. **GST treatment** — Digital products may attract GST; tax treatment to be confirmed.
3. **Refund policy** — Digital product refund terms need legal review.
4. **Privacy notice** — Data processing, retention, deletion policies need review.
5. **Customer support obligations** — Response time commitments, support channels.
6. **Data protection** — Indian data protection requirements (DPDP Act) compliance.
7. **Payment gateway terms** — Razorpay merchant agreement terms.
8. **Email communications** — Transactional email compliance (opt-out not required for transactional).

## Technical Foundations Implemented

- RLS policies for data isolation
- Server-side authorization for downloads
- No sensitive data in logs
- HMAC signature verification for webhooks
- Short-lived signed URLs for downloads
- Environment variable validation

## Not Claimed

- No legal certification claimed
- No guaranteed compliance asserted
- No fake business registration numbers created
