# CareerVault Research Policy

## Research Principles

All external factual claims must be traceable and verifiable. Research must prioritise authoritative sources and avoid invention or speculation.

## Source Requirements

For any external factual claim, record:
- **Source name** - The name of the publication, company, or organisation
- **Source URL** - The direct link to the source material
- **Publication or access date** - When the content was published or accessed
- **The claim supported** - Which specific claim the source validates
- **Last verification date** - When the claim was last checked

## Priority Sources (in order)

1. **Official company career pages** - Company career sites, official hiring documentation
2. **Employer documentation** - Official HR guides, employment contracts, company policies
3. **Government sources** - Labour department publications, government career services, immigration/visa documentation
4. **Authoritative professional references** - recognised professional bodies, industry standard guides

## Sources to Avoid or Use with Extreme Caution

- Third-party salary survey sites without primary validation
- Forum discussions or informal community sharing
- Single-source claims without corroboration
- Content behind paywalls without access verification
- Outdated information (verify current norms)

## Prohibited Practices

- Inventing ATS scoring percentages or guaranteed scores
- Fabricating salary increase percentages
- Inventing interview success rates
- Creating company-specific hiring questions
- Inventing customer testimonials or results
- Claiming universal ATS rejection based on single scores or keyword formulas
- Presenting estimates as official calculations

## Verification Requirements

- All salary data must include explicit assumptions and allow user editing
- All interview guidance must be based on general best practices, not company-specific insider information
- All resume advice must be applicant-friendly and truthful
- Any claimed industry norms must be traceable to at least one verifiable source

## Documentation Standard

Every research claim in any resource must have a corresponding reference entry following the format above. Resources lacking proper source documentation should be marked as "draft" review status until verification is complete.
